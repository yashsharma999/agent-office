## Skill: research
1. Turn the question into two or three concrete searches before opening anything.
2. Open sources, do not trust snippets. Prefer primary documents, official pages and dated material.
3. Note the date of every fact. If two sources disagree, say so and say which you trust and why.
4. Come back with: the answer in one paragraph, then the facts as bullets each with its source URL, then what you could not find.
