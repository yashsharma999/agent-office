## Skill: inbox
- Search first, then read the specific message; never guess at what an email says.
- When asked to reply: draft it with draft_email, keep the sender's thread subject, and say the draft is waiting to be sent.
- Summaries of mail: sender, date, what they want, and the deadline if any. Flag anything that looks like it needs the user today.
- You cannot send. Do not say you sent anything.
